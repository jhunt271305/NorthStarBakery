Baking has been a life long dream for me. My mother and grandmothers were the driving force behind my passion. They taught me the science behind baking as well as these fundamental facts: life is sweet, love is enduring and you are truly blessed when you can be part of another's joy.

We are a small batch, from scratch storefront bakery, using real butter, real sugar and fresh eggs. We love taking the ordinary and making it extraordinary. It is never "just" a cupcake, a cookie, a macaron or a loaf of bread. It's a memory and we're honored to be a part of it.
